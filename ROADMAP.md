# CompleteOS+ V1 Roadmap

## Current Mission

Ship a stable, production-ready V1 of CompleteOS+.

The goal of V1 is not feature completeness.

The goal is a reliable personal operating system that allows users to plan, organize, and execute their lives.

Prefer simplicity over feature count.

Do not add features unless they contribute directly to execution.

V2 ideas should remain out of scope unless explicitly requested.
